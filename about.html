About Rabbit Notes 🐰

A simple and efficient note-taking app built with the latest technologies

Why Rabbit Notes?

Rabbit Notes is a note-taking application that allows you to create and organize your notes with ease. Unlike other apps that store your data on external servers, your notes are saved locally in your browser's storage (localStorage). This means your data stays private and secure on your device, offering you a higher level of control and security.

Since your data never leaves your device, you can trust that your information is not being shared or stored anywhere else. This makes Rabbit Notes a safer choice for those who value privacy.

Technologies Used

This application is built with the following technologies : HTML , Tailwind CSS and TypeScript
